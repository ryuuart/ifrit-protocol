# Shadow play

Forms standing on a pale ground before a grey back wall, under three
lights that each cast a shadow:

- a **sun**, high and warm, turning once round the set each loop, its
  shadow drawn as three cascades along the camera's depth so the front of
  the set is held at a finer texel than the wall;
- a cool **spot** circling above the front of the set the other way,
  always aimed at the middle of the ground, its shadows filtered a little
  softer than the others;
- an amber **lamp** standing low among the forms, whose shadows reach
  every way from it, its bulb glowing and blocking nothing.

The forms: a red column, a blue ball resting on the ground, a gold ring
held in the air and turning, so its shadow stands apart from it, a green
slab, and an arc of seven stones drawn as one instanced body, every stone
casting its own shadow.

Two bodies show what a set can say about who casts:

- the **green slab** is left out of the spot's shadow link, so it casts the
  sun's and the lamp's shadows and never the spot's — the spot's light
  passes straight through where it stands;
- the **pale floating cube** says it casts no shadow at all, so it is lit
  and shaded like any other body and darkens nothing beneath it.

The loop is 8 seconds and closes on itself: the sun and the spot sweep a
full turn each, and the ring turns.

What it does not do: shadows are opaque or absent, so nothing casts a
coloured or partial shadow; a shadow's softness is the filter's and does
not widen with the distance from its caster; and nothing is interactive.
