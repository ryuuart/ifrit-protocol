# Semagram Lab

An original, animated workstation inspired by *Arrival*: a suspended ink
sentence, a polar measurement frame, acoustic traces and a set of morphological
neighbours. The annotations are invented research notes; they do not claim to
translate the film's logograms.

The main drawing and each fragment use the native pen's seeded noise and random
stream. Their textures are retained. A file-backed material samples the library's
noise field to modulate the main drawing's alpha, while a bound scan line moves
through the observation area. The polar marks and ink contours use the geometry
library's ellipse arrangement.

The canvas is 1440 × 960. Its catalogue still is at 4.8 seconds. The ink suspension
has a 16-second cycle and the measurement scan has a 9-second cycle.

Reference direction: circular ink, irregular deposition, pale atmosphere and
linguistic uncertainty. The composition, drawings and prose are original.
