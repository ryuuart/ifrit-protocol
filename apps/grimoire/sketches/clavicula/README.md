# Clavicula — a grand pentacle

A native grimoire plate based on **Wellcome MS.4659**, a mid-eighteenth-century
French *Key of Solomon*. The main figure follows the grand pentacle on
manuscript page 126: two squares, eight cross-tipped points, two inscription
circles and a stepped central cross. The title leaf supplies the surrounding
zodiac ring; the smaller diagrams quote the square-and-circle structure of
the manuscript's circle on page 123.

This is a composed interpretation of those pages. Enlarged type, extra ruled
divisions, the outer divine-name ring, red ink, gilding, the marginal rose and
moving illumination are additions. It is not a diplomatic transcription or a
facsimile of a single leaf. The source uses brown ink on paper; its pentacle is
not gilded.

All artwork is native geometry, shaped text and material. The paper and fixed
ink are retained together. A small lit surface supplies the moving reflection,
painted directly on the gilded contours in page coordinates. A short highlight
walks both square contours by arc length while the inscriptions remain fixed.
The title uses Snell Roundhand, supporting text Baskerville, and zodiac symbols
Apple Symbols, with explicit fallbacks.

Open `clavicula.cpp` in Grimoire. Its declared still is at five seconds.

Reference: [Pseudo-Solomon, MS.4659 — Wellcome Collection](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/hwqdep8w).
The collection marks the digitized manuscript public domain. The reference
images are used for comparison, and are not bundled in this sketch.
