# Light table

A 1440 × 1080 Compose material inspection desk: satin aluminium, raised gold
letters, an enamel brush deposit, copper pressure bands and tilted normal
coupons. The letters use flatter brushed normals than the plate. Every material
paints its colour stack flat; the metallic, roughness, normal and coating
channels describe the surface without shading it. There are no cast shadows or
depth.

Below the specimen, paired cards compare flat ink with raised letters, and a
cached picture with live content.
