# Soft boxes

A row of five steel balls from a mirror to a matte, and a lacquered ball
under a clear coat, on a glossy ground, lit only by lights with a size:

- a large **rect** softbox above and behind the row, tipped toward it and
  toward the eye — the one light that casts, from its centre;
- a narrow upright **strip**, a rect light at the front left;
- a warm **sphere** light low at the right;
- a round **disk** light overhead at the back left.

Each light is seen as a panel or a ball of its own shape and colour,
standing where the light stands. The ground mirrors the softbox as a
rectangle and the strip as a band, and the balls carry the softbox along
their tops — a small sharp outline on the mirror, a broad smear on the
rough ones.

The loop is 8 seconds and closes on itself. The softbox glides across the
back of the row and returns, turning a little toward where it is going,
so its rectangle slides along the ground and over the balls' tops.

There is no environment, so a mirror shows only the lights; the ground
reflects the lights and nothing else. The shadows are the softbox's alone
and no softer for its size, and nothing is interactive.
